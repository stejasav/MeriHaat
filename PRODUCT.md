# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
React.js (JavaScript, not TypeScript) + Tailwind CSS, bundled with Vite. Mandated by the user; no Next.js, no TypeScript.

## Users
Urban families in Indian metros (Delhi NCR first) who plan the household's fresh food a day ahead: ordering fruit, vegetables, dairy and daily essentials in the evening for next-morning doorstep delivery. Some will run recurring orders (milk, a weekly fruit box).

## Product Purpose
MeriHaat ("my market") brings the trusted neighbourhood haat online: hand-picked fresh produce and everyday essentials, sourced close to the farm, delivered early the next morning. Success is a family that stops visiting the mandi because the produce arriving at the door is reliably better.

## Positioning
A curated haat, not an endless supermarket: fewer, better items chosen daily, honest pricing in INR, and next-morning delivery. Starts with fruit and expands into vegetables, dairy, staples and other essentials, so the brand must not be fruit-only.

## Operating Context
Evening ordering on phones for a morning delivery slot; cart built from a few categories; quantity chosen per unit (kg, g, piece, dozen, litre).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Browse categories and collections, search, filter by category, choose quantity, functional cart (client-side).
- Bestsellers and recommendations, testimonials, trust sections, footer.
- No backend yet: catalogue, cart and checkout are front-end only. Checkout/payment is out of scope.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: MeriHaat. Wordmark to be designed; no existing logo.
- References chosen by the user: delhifruitmarket.com and countrydelight.in, to be combined, not copied.
- Must feel authentic, trustworthy, premium, welcoming; not a generic template.

## Evidence on Hand
- No real testimonials, customer counts, certifications or press. All such content is SAMPLE content, kept in one editable data file.
- Photography: Unsplash stock (hotlinked) as stand-ins for future own shoots.

## Product Principles
1. Freshness is the product: every surface should make provenance and timing (picked, delivered) legible.
2. Honest prices: INR, per-unit, discounts shown plainly.
3. Fewer, better choices over endless catalogue.
4. Built to grow beyond fruit into the full daily basket.
