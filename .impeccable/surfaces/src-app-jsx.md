# Surface: MeriHaat storefront (home, shop, cart drawer)

Mode: Persuade (home) with Operate shop/cart flows inside the same world.
Audience: urban families in Delhi NCR ordering tonight for next-morning delivery. Action: add to basket. Proof: provenance (origin per product), season, delivery-by-date promise, sample testimonials (labelled sample in data).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The storefront is tomorrow's leaf of a household wall calendar: the delivery date is the hero, the season decides the shelf. Refuses the photo-carousel hero plus round category chips that both references ship.

OWN-WORLD: Two-ink calendar print on off-white stock (#f6f5f1): carbon ink #17140f rules and type, vermilion #e0301e only for what is committed (tomorrow's date, basket, active month, discounts), leaf green #1f4d2b for season/origin. Archivo across widths: ultra-condensed black numerals, normal-width UI. Tiro Devanagari Hindi for Hindi names. Ruled 1-2px ink grid; square corners; real photography is the only full colour.

STORY: The visitor sees which date their haat lands, what is in season now and from where, fills a basket with honest per-unit INR prices, and trusts the process (cut-off, picking, cold chain, sample voices).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ink announcement strip (delivery area, cut-off). Header: wordmark, search, category links, vermilion basket with total. Left third: date leaf, month caps, giant vermilion day numeral of tomorrow, weekday, live countdown to 11 PM cut-off, ink CTA. Right two-thirds: 'In season this week' 4x2 ruled grid of working product cells (origin kicker, photo, name, price, Add/stepper). Green season band across the bottom: months, current one in vermilion with its produce.

FORM: Household wall calendar / tear-off date pad; candidate 7 of 7. seed:b2d4ae570c8d66d1. Signature interaction: adding to basket stamps the cell with a vermilion tick and the date leaf's basket count ticks; the season band filters the shelf by month.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
